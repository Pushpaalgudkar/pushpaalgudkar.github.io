About

A student who learns by building.

I am studying Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at Dayananda Sagar University in Ramanagar, Karnataka. I came into this branch through branch entry, which meant catching up quickly — and that turned out to be the part I enjoyed most.

Right now my focus is the groundwork: writing C and Python properly, understanding how robotics and machine learning actually work rather than just what they are called, and using small projects to check whether I have really understood something. I am also interning with the Bosch Rexroth Centre at my university.

Outside coursework I edit video, sketch and doodle digitally, and crochet. I like that these sit next to the engineering work rather than away from it — a lot of what I build ends up somewhere between the two.

Projects

Things I'm building.

Both of these are works in progress. I am building them while I learn, so they change often — but they are the clearest picture of what I am actually doing right now.

In progress

Fluffy Knots

A website for my crochet work — plushies, flowers, pouches and keychains — built so the pieces I make by hand have somewhere to live. It has a filterable product grid with search, quick view, a basket that hands over to WhatsApp, a wishlist, a light and dark theme, and a how-it's-made section. I am building it with the web course I am working through, so it doubles as practice: every new thing I learn goes straight into it.

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • Crochet

In progress

Birthday surprise site

A little site made for one person: a friend opens it on their birthday, types in a passcode only they would know, and the page unlocks a set of surprises — a letter, memories you scratch off to reveal, and a bouquet you build yourself by dragging flowers onto the page. It is the project where I have learned the most about making a page respond to what someone does, rather than just sit there.

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • Canvas
